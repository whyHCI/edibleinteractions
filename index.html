Schedule

9:00 – 9:30 | Welcome & Introduction

Welcome to the workshop and the wonderful people in the room!

Quick intro to the theme, the day, and each other.

9:30 – 10:15 | Part 1: Quick Fire YES or NO! — Is It an Interface?

A fast, playful game exploring:

  • What makes something an interface?
  • What changes when you can eat it?
  • Is eating interaction... or destruction?
  • What happens when the interface becomes part of your body?

10:15 – 10:45 | Coffee Break

10:45 – 12:30 | Part 2: Showcasing — EAT IT!

Hands-on demos of edible interaction prototypes, including short presentations, experiences, and Q&A.

12:30 – 13:30 | Lunch Break

13:30 – 16:00 | Part 3: Design Exercise — Edible Journey

Group activity imagining and designing edible interaction journeys using the Edible Journey Map.

16:00 – 17:00 | Wrap-Up & Networking

Open demo walkthrough, playful exploration, chats, and making new connections.

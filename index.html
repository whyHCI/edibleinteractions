About

This workshop convenes perspectives from HCI, fabrication, perceptual science, and materials science to build community, align knowledge, and articulate future directions for the field. Through multisensory demonstrations and hands-on activities, we aim to balance technological interventions with human factors to chart the future of edible interactions.

Call for Participation

What if food is not only something we eat, but a medium through which we interact with the intertwined digital and material world? Join our DIS2026 workshop, Beyond Interfaces: The Future of Edible Interactions. Expect a mix of making, playing, thinking, tasting, and imagining together.

Edible interactions are emerging as an exciting direction in HCI, grounded within Human-Food Interaction (HFI), where food is no longer just a source of sustenance. Instead, it becomes an interactive interface with computing technologies, a testbed of research, and a material of design. In this workshop, we will dive into this evolving space together, exploring how edible interactions can be sensed, transformed, and experienced across multiple modalities – touch, taste, sound, and beyond.

✨ About the Workshop

This hands-on workshop brings together people working across HCI, design, engineering, perception science, and more. We will:

  • Experience live demos & prototypes
  • Engage in interactive group discussions
  • Share early ideas, concepts, and artifacts
  • Co-create future directions for edible interactions

👥 Who we're looking for

We welcome:

  • Researchers & students in HCI, design, fabrication, or engineering
  • People working on multisensory, crossmodal, or material-based experiences
  • Anyone exploring food, perception, or interactive systems

🚀 What we aim to achieve

Together, we want to:

  • Build a connected community around edible interactions
  • Develop a shared language and framework across disciplines
  • Explore future applications and research directions
  • Spark new collaborations and projects

How to Participate

To participate, please submit a 1–2 page position paper in PDF format using the ACM Master Article Template. Your submission should briefly introduce your background, explain your interest in edible interactions, and describe what you would like to contribute to the workshop. Optional links to a demo video or project page are welcome. We also encourage alternative forms of submission, such as images, sketches, videos, or other formats that help communicate your ideas.

The deadline to express interest in attending is: 30th, May.

All submissions will be reviewed by the workshop organizers. Participants will be selected based on the relevance of their contribution, the diversity of perspectives they bring, and their potential to support constructive discussion during the workshop. Accepted contributions will be shared with all participants in advance of the event.

If accepted, at least one author of each submission must attend the workshop at DIS 2026.

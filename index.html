Beyond Interfaces: the Future of Edible Interactions

Designing food as an interactive, multisensory interface. A community workshop for HCI, fabrication, perception, and culinary creativity.

Sunday, 14 June 2026 DIS 2026 (UTown at the National University of Singapore)

About

This workshop convenes perspectives from HCI, fabrication, perceptual science, and materials science to build community, align knowledge, and articulate future directions for the field. Through multisensory demonstrations and hands-on activities, we aim to balance technological interventions with human factors to chart the future of edible interactions.

Call for Participation

This one-day workshop welcomes researchers, designers, artists, and culinary practitioners who are interested in edible interactions. We invite participants from diverse disciplinary backgrounds to join us in exploring how edible materials can function as interfaces, interactive media, and design materials.

Relevant topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Design: Design thinking, participatory and speculative approaches, and design methodologies for developing edible interfaces.
  • Materials and Fabrication: The current repertoire of edible materials, their properties and functionalities, interaction mechanisms, and approaches to fabrication and prototyping.
  • Interactive Technologies: Technologies that enable edible interactions, including XR, AI, wearable systems, computational techniques, and shape-changing interfaces, etc.
  • User Experiences: Multisensory interaction and augmentation, including crossmodal perception, sensation, and embodied eating experiences.
  • Evaluative Frames: Ways of knowing and evaluating edible interactions, including qualitative and quantitative methods, as well as psychophysical measures.
  • Application Scenarios: Potential application areas, including
    • Health and well-being: accessibility, inclusiveness, dietary needs, and supportive interventions;
    • Consumption and culture: dietary practices, perception, sociality, commensality, and cultural or ethical considerations;
    • Engagement and futures: entertainment, play, telepresence, and real-world deployment contexts.

To participate, please submit a 1–2 page position paper in PDF format using the ACM Master Article Template. Your submission should briefly introduce your background, explain your interest in edible interactions, and describe what you would like to contribute to the workshop. Optional links to a demo video or project page are welcome. We also encourage alternative forms of submission, such as images, sketches, videos, or other formats that help communicate your ideas.

The deadline to express interest in attending is: Submissions will be reviewed on a rolling basis.

All submissions will be reviewed by the workshop organizers. Participants will be selected based on the relevance of their contribution, the diversity of perspectives they bring, and their potential to support constructive discussion during the workshop. Accepted contributions will be shared with all participants in advance of the event.

If accepted, at least one author of each submission must attend the workshop at DIS 2026.
